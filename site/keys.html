05

Write and reply

A new echomail message is addressed to All. Netmail needs the recipient's FTN address in the To address field before it saves. A reply starts with Hello and the author's first name, then the quoted body with their initials (Alice Bob gives AB>), without the tearline and Origin. Cmd+S reaches the app only in terminals that pass it on; F2 and Ctrl+S always work.

KeyAction
F2 Ctrl+S Cmd+SSave the message
↑ ↓Move between From, To, Subj and the body; ↑ on the body's top line goes to Subj
EscCancel; asks before discarding the draft
