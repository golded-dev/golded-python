Blue screens. On purpose.

golded --theme dark|light

Screens.

Each screen at 80×25 from the demo base, in both themes. Rendered by Textual from the running app, not drawn by hand.

00

Splash

Product, version and licence, with a countdown. Any key continues.

01

Area list

Areas in the base with their message counts. Letters filter the list.

02

Message list

The THREADED demo area as a thread tree (t). The pagebar in the right border shows the position.

03

Message view

Header with message number, From, To and Subj, then the body. The status bar shows how many messages are left.

04

Reply

r opens a draft with the greeting and the quoted body. F2 saves it.
